# Product requirements

**Status: Planned; no business workflows are implemented in Milestone 1.**

The MVP covers cooperative management, multi-cooperative farmer membership, livestock registration and passports, identity and ownership evidence, veterinary records, breeding, milk production, interventions, dashboards, reports, and audit history.

Farmers may be registered without accounts. Veterinarians have independent professional accounts and require explicit, scoped authorization before accessing cooperative or farmer records. Farmer-reported, officer-recorded, system-recorded, and professionally verified information remain distinct.

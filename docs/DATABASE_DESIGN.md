# Database design

**Status: Milestone 2C animal identity and cooperative links are implemented in addition to the identity, tenancy, consent, professional authorization and integrity baseline. Ownership and later record modules remain planned.**

Implemented in `prisma/schema.prisma`: User, Party, Cooperative, Farmer, FarmerPrivateContact, FarmerCooperativeMembership, FarmerUserLink, RoleAssignment, FarmerConsent, ConsentRevision, ConsentRevocation, RecordAccessGrant, ProfessionalProfile, ProfessionalCredentialEvidence, ProfessionalCredentialReview, ProfessionalAuthorization, ProfessionalAuthorizationDecision, and ProfessionalAuthorizationScope. The incremental migrations also include `20261126110000_consent_capture_provenance` and `20261126130000_consent_and_authorization_integrity`.

Milestone 2C adds Animal, AnimalIdentifier and CooperativeAnimalLink. Milestone 2C.1 makes identifier and link history immutable, terminal when retired/ended, and non-deletable. External identifier namespaces may be reused after retirement; Livestock Trust `INTERNAL_REFERENCE` values are permanently unique. Membership-backed active links require an active membership. The animal professional-scope foreign key remains, while a database check rejects all `ANIMAL` scopes; runtime animal authorization is disabled in application policy and at the database boundary. Consent provenance and professional review/decision history are immutable. Still planned: Auth.js adapter tables, custody/location, ownership, veterinary, breeding, milk, intervention, revision and audit modules.

Farmer-to-cooperative membership is many-to-many. Consent farmer, cooperative, scope type and scope reference are immutable identity fields; revisions amend capture metadata and dates, while scope changes require a new consent ID. Consent records distinguish the farmer or representative provider from the recording field officer, with purpose and evidence/attestation reference fields. Legacy rows are `LEGACY_UNVERIFIED` and cannot authorize new grants. Revoked consent and grants are terminal at the database boundary. Legacy professional profiles without review history and active authorizations without decision history are quarantined without deleting evidence, and required review/decision history cannot be deleted while authority is active. Protected reads use `src/lib/authz/protected-data-service.ts`. Animal ownership remains planned for a later migration, and animal professional authorization is rejected at both service and database levels until that module exists.
